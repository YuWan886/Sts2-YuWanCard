## Short Description

YuWan Card is a content-rich mod for Slay the Spire 2 that adds the Pig character along with 150+ cards, 90+ powers, 90+ relics, 6 enchantments, an Ancient, custom monsters and a boss, 6 events, 4 modifiers, orbs, potions, and badges. Highlights include a 60+ card colorless pool, the What If relic series, the Malice difficulty system (0-10, independent of Ascension), a Balatro mode, an Endless mode, the Ring of Seven Curses, dedicated multiplayer cards and relics, and Hextech cross-mod runes.

## Full Description

[h1]YuWanCard - A Feature-Rich Pig-Themed Mod for Slay the Spire 2![/h1]

[h2]Core Features at a Glance:[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]🐷 Character – Pig[/b]: Fully playable character with 80 starting HP, able to use cards of all colors; exclusive pig-themed starter deck and relic, independent card pool and relic pool; custom portraits, animations, shop/rest site art, and the "Pig Tycoon" achievement badge.

[*][b]🃏 Colorless Card Pool[/b]: Over 60 colorless cards usable by all characters, bringing more variety and unique mechanics to each run.

[*][b]⚙️ "What If" Series Relics[/b]: 40+ rule-breaking relics, each rewriting a core game rule (e.g., "Only Ancient Cards," "All Enemies Become Elites," "Only Attack Cards," "Infinite Upgrades," "Double Map Length," etc.), toggleable in mod settings.

[*][b]💀 Malice Difficulty (0–10)[/b]: An independent difficulty system separate from Ascension, with progress saved per character. Enemies gain random affixes at combat start (36 in total, graded by rarity), unlocking progressively with Malice level; higher levels increase enemy HP and damage, and add affix slots for Normal/Elite/Boss enemies; defeating Elites and Bosses can drop exclusive Seven Deadly Sins relics.

[*][b]🃏 Balatro Mode[/b]: Brings Balatro-style mechanics into the Spire, including combo multipliers, interest, and card modifiers, unlocking exclusive Balatro cards (Tarot, Planet cards) and Joker relics.

[*][b]♾️ Endless Mode[/b]: Climb the Spire without end until death; enemies grow stronger with each cycle, and you gain bonus max HP when choosing this challenge.

[*][b]💍 Ring of Seven Curses[/b]: A high-risk, high-reward core ancient relic playstyle, providing extra Energy, card draw, and Boss damage at the cost of Gold, Block, and healing; built around it is a dedicated relic ecosystem, including curse-synergy relics that only appear while wearing it.

[*][b]👥 Multiplayer Cards and Relics[/b]: Cooperative content designed for team play (revive allies, sacrifice HP, turn allies into pigs, shared forge values, etc.); multiplayer relics like "Teammate Pays" and a shared shopping cart system.

[*][b]🎲 Events and Monsters[/b]: 6 events (including Smithy upgrade/fusion cards, Land's End, etc.); custom enemies – Killer Elite, Flame Demon Boss.

[*][b]📦 More Content[/b]: 6 enchantments, 2 custom orbs, 1 healing potion, rest site options; Ancient Folk (Piggy), Vakku Tower Entry modifier (AI auto-play cards); totaling [b]150+ cards / 90+ powers / 90+ relics[/b].
[/list]

[h1]YuWanCard - 特色丰富的《杀戮尖塔 2》猪主题模组！[/h1]

[h2]核心特色一览：[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]🐷 角色 – 猪[/b]：完整可玩角色，起始生命 80，可使用所有颜色卡牌；专属猪主题起始牌组与遗物，独立卡牌池与遗物池；自定义立绘、动画、商店/休息处美术，以及“养猪大亨”成就徽章。

[*][b]🃏 无色卡牌池[/b]：超过 60 张无色卡牌，所有角色皆可使用，为对局带来更多变化与独特机制。

[*][b]⚙️ “假如”系列遗物[/b]：40+ 个颠覆规则的遗物，每个都改写一条核心规则（如“只有先古牌”“小怪全变精英”“只有攻击牌”“无限升级”“地图长度翻倍”等），可在模组设置中开关。

[*][b]💀 恶意难度（0–10）[/b]：独立于进阶的难度系统，按角色独立存档。敌人在战斗开始时获得随机词条（共 36 个，按稀有度分级），随恶意等级逐步解锁；更高等级提升敌人生命与伤害，并为普通/精英/Boss 增加词条槽位；击败精英与 Boss 可掉落专属七宗罪罪孽遗物。

[*][b]🃏 Balatro 模式[/b]：将 Balatro 风格带入尖塔，包含连击乘数、利息与卡牌修饰器，解锁专属 Balatro 卡牌（塔罗牌、星球牌）以及小丑遗物。

[*][b]♾️ 无尽模式[/b]：爬塔无止境，直至死亡；敌人随每次循环不断增强，选择该挑战时获得额外最大生命。

[*][b]💍 七咒之戒[/b]：高风险高回报的先古遗物核心流派，提供额外能量、抽牌与 Boss 伤害，代价是金币、格挡与治疗；围绕它构建了专属遗物生态，包括仅在佩戴时出现的诅咒协同遗物。

[*][b]👥 多人联机卡牌与遗物[/b]：为团队协作打造的合作内容（复活队友、献祭生命、变队友为猪、共享锻造值等）；多人遗物如“队友帮付”及共享购物车系统。

[*][b]🎲 事件与怪物[/b]：6 个事件（含铁匠铺升级/融合卡牌、天涯海角等）；自定义敌人——杀手精英、焰魔 Boss。

[*][b]📦 更多内容[/b]：6 个附魔、2 种自定义充能球、1 瓶治疗药水、休息处选项；先古之民（猪猪）、瓦库进塔修饰符（AI 自动托管出牌）；总计 [b]150+ 张卡牌 / 90+ 个能力 / 90+ 个遗物[/b]。
[/list]
